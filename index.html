About

I am a post-doctoral researcher at Université Grenoble Alpes (GETALP / LIG), working at the intersection of speech processing, natural language processing, and sign language technologies.

My current research focuses on multimodal AI for under-resourced languages and communication modalities — in particular, automatic translation and recognition of French Sign Language (LSF), pictogram generation from text and speech for augmentative and alternative communication (AAC), and self-supervised representation learning for French text and audio.

Before my postdoc, my research centred on articulatory speech processing: I developed deep learning methods for tongue tracking from ultrasound images and built visual articulatory biofeedback systems for speech therapy — applied clinically to patients with post-stroke aphasia and post-glossectomy speech disorders. This background gives me a strong motivation for building AI with tangible real-world impact, particularly in health and accessibility.

I am based in Grenoble.
