About

I am a post-doctoral researcher in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at Université Grenoble Alpes. My research focuses on developing robust and interpretable machine learning methods for complex real-world data.

My work sits at the interface of statistical learning theory and practical applications, with a particular interest in scenarios where data is scarce, heterogeneous, or comes with structured uncertainty. I am passionate about making AI systems that scientists and practitioners can genuinely trust.
